# Workshop notes — BookSwap (internal, 12 Oct)

- Students at our university buy expensive textbooks every semester and resell them badly (WhatsApp groups, flyers).
- Idea: a web app where students publish books they no longer need (title, course, condition) and request swaps with other students.
- Only the owner can remove their listing. Title is mandatory.
- Search by course is a must; search by ISBN "would be nice".
- Nobody knows how many swaps happen today. Survey of 40 students said "most" struggle to find the right book (no numbers kept).
- Payments, shipping and a mobile app were mentioned; the dean said "not now".
- Pilot with 2 faculties in the next semester. Owner: Ana (product), dev lead: Rui.
