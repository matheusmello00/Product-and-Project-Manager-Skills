# user-interview-prep

A topic or hypothesis in, a printable interview kit out: one research goal, 3–5 falsifiable hypotheses, a 30-minute guide with non-leading questions and follow-ups, behavioural signals to watch, anti-patterns, a screener note and a consent/recording note.

**When to use:** before discovery interviews, to understand a metric drop, to explore a segment, or to prepare follow-ups after feedback analysis.
**Not for:** running interviews, synthesising findings or validating a solution (use feedback clustering / synthesis skills).

## Requirements
Nothing. Optional: local files with feedback, personas or prior research passed as context. No script, no connector.

## How to use
1. Give the topic or hypothesis (and the segment if you know it).
2. Optionally attach context files.
3. Receive `interview-brief-[topic].md`, two pages max.

## Behaviour notes
- The goal always starts with **Understand** or **Discover**; solutions are reframed into the problem behind them.
- Each hypothesis has a "true if" and a "false if"; weak ones are called out.
- Maximum 12 questions, none starting with "Would you…" or "Do you think…", none about your solution; every core question has at least 2 follow-ups.
- Context (feedback, personas) sharpens hypotheses but is cited, never presented as fact.
- Consent and recording are always flagged.

## Language
Default to English. Always reply in the language the user writes in or explicitly requests (any language, e.g. Portuguese, Spanish). Only the output language changes, not the logic. Verbatim items (quotes, names, product terms) are kept untouched.
